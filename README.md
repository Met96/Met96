### 📱 My app: Deyz

**[Deyz](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/deyz/id6758526967)** turns the dates that matter into countdowns, and tracks the days since the moments you want to remember. Home Screen widgets, calendar integration, a minimal black-and-white design, and **no data collected**. Built with accessibility in mind: VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text and Reduced Motion.

Designed, built and shipped by me, from idea to the App Store.
